FAQ

JPG or PNG for thumbnails?

Both work. JPG is smaller; PNG is better for text-heavy thumbnails with flat colors. Switch the output format above if you prefer PNG.

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. ExactKB runs entirely in your browser. Your file never leaves your device — this page even works offline.
