FAQ

My photo keeps getting rejected even under 50 KB. Why?

Usually the dimensions — SSC expects roughly 200×230 px — or the file being under the 20 KB minimum. This preset fixes all three at once.

Does this work for CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD?

The 20–50 KB / 10–20 KB pattern is standard across SSC recruitments, but verify your notification's annexure to be sure.

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. ExactKB runs entirely in your browser. Your file never leaves your device — this page even works offline.
