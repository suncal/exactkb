Who uses Request Kits

  • Coaching centres & consultants — send every student the exam's exact photo/signature kit instead of a printed instruction sheet.
  • HR & admissions — collect compliant documents on the first try, not the third.
  • Families — build the kit once, send it to a parent; they tap, drop, done.
  • Website owners — embed a kit directly on your page with the iframe snippet.

How it works — and why it's private

The entire kit — every requirement you set — is encoded inside the link itself. There is no server, no database, and no account. When someone opens your kit, their files are processed inside their own browser and never uploaded anywhere. You're not collecting files; you're making sure the files they submit (to the portal, to you by email, anywhere) are right the first time.
