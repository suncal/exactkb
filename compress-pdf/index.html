FAQ

Will the text still be readable?

Yes — ExactKB uses the highest quality your KB budget allows. At very tight limits on long documents, expect softer text; the tool warns you if a limit is unreachable.

Does the text stay selectable?

No — pages are re-rendered as images to guarantee the size target, which is what upload portals care about. Keep your original for archival.

How many pages can it handle?

Up to 60 pages. For bigger files, split the PDF first.

Is my document uploaded to a server?

No. The PDF engine runs entirely in your browser — certificates, statements and ID scans never leave your device.
