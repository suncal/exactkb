FAQ

Why does my DP look blurry?

WhatsApp aggressively recompresses large uploads. A pre-sized 640×640 file under 500 KB needs minimal recompression and stays sharp.

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. ExactKB runs entirely in your browser. Your file never leaves your device — this page even works offline.
